Chrome extension · version 0.4.4

Capture the whole page.
Not just what you can see.

Kapture is a Chrome extension that screenshots a complete webpage, scrolling the whole document, not only the visible viewport. Save it as PNG, JPEG or PDF, straight to your Downloads folder.

Add to Chrome Add to Mac See how it works

Requires Chrome 120 or later. Screenshots never leave your device. Dedicated Mac app coming soon.

Two ways to capture

Everything, or exactly
one part of it.

Full page

Kapture scrolls the main document and captures it end to end, splitting very long pages into 8,000 pixel sections so the files stay usable.

Select area

Drag over the visible page to grab one region. Choose Freeform, 1:1, 16:9 or 9:16, then move or resize the selection before you capture.

What you get

Built for the screenshot you actually needed.

PNG, JPEG or PDF

Pick the format before you capture. PDF produces one local, multi‑page file.

Viewport presets

Capture at your current window, or render the page at Phone 390, Tablet 820, Desktop 1440 or Desktop 1920 first.

Save where it makes sense

Send captures straight to Downloads, or name a folder per page or project to keep them organised.

Local history

Newest first, with the site and time. Open a screenshot, or select entries and delete the files for good.

Light and dark

The side panel has both themes, and remembers the one you chose.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no ads, no analytics. Install it and capture.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

  1. 1

    Open the page

    Go to whatever you want to keep.

  2. 2

    Click Kapture

    The side panel opens beside the page.

  3. 3

    Capture

    Choose full page or an area, then save.

Privacy

Your screenshots
stay on your device.

Kapture processes the page on your computer and saves the file through Chrome's own Downloads system. Screenshots, webpage content, URLs and history are not sent to the developer or to any third party, and Kapture contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

  • No account required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No remote code
  • Files saved locally by Chrome
