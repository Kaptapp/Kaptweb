Kapture for Chrome · 0.4.7

Everything, or exactly
one part of it.

Two capture modes, four file destinations, and nothing that leaves your machine.

Full page

Kapture scrolls the main document and captures it end to end, splitting very long pages into 8,000 pixel sections so the files stay usable.

Select area

Drag over the visible page to grab one region. Choose Freeform, 1:1, 16:9 or 9:16, then move or resize the selection before you capture.

In the extension

Built for the screenshot you actually needed.

PNG, JPEG or PDF

Pick the format before you capture. PDF produces one local, multi‑page file.

Viewport presets

Capture at your current window, or render the page at Phone 390, Tablet 820, Desktop 1440 or Desktop 1920 first.

Project folders

Name a folder per page or project. Captures land there in sequence, ready for Kapture Pro to pick up.

History previews

Newest first, with a preview, the site and the time. Open a capture, or select several and delete them for good.

Light and dark

The side panel has both themes, and remembers the one you chose.

Local only

No account, no ads, no analytics. Captures are made on your machine and stay there.

Kapture Pro for Mac

Your screenshots,
finally organised.

Kapture Pro indexes the screenshots already saved on your Mac and turns them into a searchable visual workspace.

  • Projects

    Every capture folder becomes a project you can move between.

  • Search

    Find a capture by name, site or project without opening Finder.

  • Visual library

    Browse everything as previews instead of filenames.

  • Inspector

    Size, format, source and capture time for whatever you select.

  • Tags and notes

    Add your own context so a capture still makes sense later.

  • Local storage

    No cloud, no sync, no account. It reads the disk you already have.

Your files stay where they already live.
Kapture Pro simply helps you find and organise them.

How it works

Chrome captures.
Mac organises.

  1. Capture

    In Chrome

    Open the side panel, pick full page or an area, and choose the project folder.

  2. Save

    To your Mac

    Files go straight to Downloads/Kapture/ and your project, named in sequence.

  3. Organise

    In Kapture Pro

    The same folder opens as a searchable library you can browse, tag and compare.

Privacy

Local by default.

Kapture creates screenshots on your computer and saves them through Chrome's own Downloads system. Kapture Pro reads the files already sitting on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, neither product needs an account, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking in either one.

  • No account required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No remote code
  • No cloud storage
