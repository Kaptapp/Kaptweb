Kapture para Chrome

Kapture para Chrome es gratis. Se distribuye a través de Chrome Web Store, cuyos términos también se aplican cuando lo instalas. Cómo trata la información se explica en nuestra Política de privacidad.

Tu cuenta de Kapture

Una cuenta de Kapture te permite iniciar sesión en kaptapp.com/account para ver tu licencia de Kapture Pro y los dispositivos en los que está activa. Inicias sesión con un enlace que te enviamos por correo electrónico. No hay contraseña, así que mantén protegido el acceso a tu cuenta de correo electrónico.

Debes tener al menos 16 años para crear una cuenta, o más si así lo exige la ley del lugar donde vives.

Para cerrar tu cuenta, escríbenos desde la dirección de correo electrónico de la cuenta. Cerrarla no borra nada de tu ordenador.

Kapture Pro

Estos términos de Kapture Pro forman parte de estos términos en todas las versiones de esta página. Por ahora solo están disponibles en inglés.

Kapture Pro adds paid features to the Kapture desktop apps for macOS and Windows. You buy it from KUMO STUDIO LIMITED, and Stripe takes the payments for us. We never see or store your full card details.

Free trial

You can start one 14-day free trial of Kapture Pro from the app once you have confirmed your email address. The trial needs no payment details and never turns into a paid plan by itself: when it ends, the Pro features stop unless you subscribe. If you subscribe during the trial, your subscription and first payment start straight away and any trial days left end.

Prices and payment

Kapture Pro costs £4.99 a month or £39.99 a year. Prices are in pounds sterling and include any VAT we have to charge. Your bank may charge a fee for paying in another currency.

Renewal

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year, and we charge the same payment method again, until you cancel.

You can switch plans in your account. Moving from monthly to annual starts a new yearly period straight away and charges the annual price less the unused part of your current month. Moving from annual to monthly takes effect when your paid year ends.

Price changes

We may change the price of Kapture Pro. We will email you at least 30 days before a new price applies to your subscription, and it applies only from your next renewal after that notice. You can cancel before then and you will not pay the new price. If the law where you live gives you more rights, you keep them.

Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time in your account at kaptapp.com/account (Manage billing), or by emailing us. Cancelling stops the next renewal: Kapture Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for, and we do not charge you again.

Kapture Pro starts as soon as you subscribe. At checkout you ask us to start it straight away and confirm that you understand your legal 14-day right to cancel ends when it starts.

In addition to your legal rights, if you are not happy with Kapture Pro, email us within 14 days of the first payment of your first subscription and we will refund that payment in full and end the subscription. Otherwise payments are not refunded for part-used periods, unless the law requires it, for example if Kapture Pro is faulty.

If a payment fails

If a renewal payment fails, Kapture Pro stays on for 3 days while Stripe tries the payment again and emails you. Update your payment method in your account. If the payment has still not gone through after 3 days, the Pro features pause until it does. If every retry fails, the subscription ends.

Payment disputes

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank. We can usually sort out a problem faster, including with a refund. While a dispute or chargeback about a Kapture Pro payment is open, we may pause Kapture Pro on your account. If the dispute is withdrawn or closed in our favour, Kapture Pro comes back automatically. If the payment is reversed, that subscription ends, and you can subscribe again at any time.

Your computers

One subscription covers up to 2 computers at a time. To use Kapture Pro on another computer, deactivate one in your account. Kapture Pro checks your subscription online at least once every 7 days and keeps working offline in between.

We may end Kapture Pro for anyone who breaks these terms, for example by sharing a subscription beyond 2 computers or interfering with its licence checks.

When Kapture Pro ends

Whether your trial or subscription ends, you cancel, or a payment fails, only the Pro features stop. Your captures, library, projects, tags and notes stay on your computer unchanged, and the free features keep working.
