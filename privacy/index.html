Kapture Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Who we are

Kapture is made by KUMO STUDIO LIMITED, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 08453597, whose registered office is at Office 2, Tweed House, Park Lane, Swanley, Kent, BR8 8DT, England. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

In short

Your captures stay on your device. We never receive your screenshots, their contents, or your library, projects, tags or notes.

Kapture for Chrome needs no account and sends nothing to us.

A Kapture account holds your email address and, if you use Kapture Pro, your licence and the devices you activate.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Kapture for Chrome

Information Kapture handles

When the user starts a capture, Kapture processes the visual content of the active webpage and its address to create the requested PNG, JPEG or PDF file. Kapture stores limited screenshot-history metadata locally in Chrome, including the filename, source website hostname, Chrome download ID, capture time and file-availability status. It also stores the user's selected theme and Downloads subfolder setting locally.

How the information is used

The information is used only to create and save screenshots, apply a user-selected viewport size, name downloaded files, display local capture history and let the user open or remove saved captures.

Local storage and retention

Screenshot files are saved directly to the user's computer through Chrome's Downloads system. History metadata and preferences are stored locally using Chrome storage. Users can clear history from Kapture and delete screenshot files through Kapture or their operating system. Local extension data is removed when the extension is uninstalled, subject to Chrome's behaviour. Downloaded screenshot files remain until the user deletes them.

Sharing and transmission

Kapture does not send webpage content, screenshots, browsing information or history metadata to the developer or to external servers. Kapture does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data services. User data is not sold or transferred to third parties.

Remote code

Kapture does not download or execute remote code. All executable code is included in the extension package distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

Chrome permissions

Kapture uses Chrome permissions only to operate its user-requested screenshot features: access the active tab, inject packaged capture controls, save and open downloads, display the side panel, store local preferences and history, and temporarily apply a selected responsive viewport.

Your Kapture account and Kapture Pro

You can create a Kapture account and sign in at kaptapp.com/account. This section explains how we handle your account information and, if you use Kapture Pro, your licence, devices and payments.

What we collect and why

Account details. Your email address, your account ID, and when you signed up and last signed in. We use them to create your account, sign you in with an emailed link and contact you about your account. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

Sign-in sessions. For each signed-in session, the IP address and browser information recorded by our sign-in provider. We use them to keep accounts secure and to stop misuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in security.

Devices. When you use Kapture Pro: an identifier created by the app, the device name, the platform (macOS or Windows), the app version, and when the device was activated, last checked its licence and was deactivated. We use them to apply the two-device limit, issue licences and show your devices on your account page. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

Licence and trial. Whether you have Kapture Pro, on which plan and until when, when a free trial started and ends, and a one-way cryptographic hash of your email address. We use them to issue licences and to offer one free trial per person. Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in preventing repeated free trials.

Payments. When you pay for Kapture Pro: your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, plan, price, status, renewal and cancellation dates, and the amount, currency, date and refund status of each payment. We use them to give you the access you pay for and to keep financial records. Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records.

Messages you send us. We use them to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to you, or our contract with you where your message is about your account.

A Kapture Pro licence saved on your computer contains your account ID, a one-way hash of the device identifier, your plan and the dates it is valid for. We sign it so the app can check it offline.

What we do not collect

We do not receive your screenshots or PDFs, their file names or contents, the pages you capture, or your library, projects, collections, tags, notes or searches. kaptapp.com, Kapture for Chrome and Kapture Pro contain no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Payments through Stripe

Payments will be handled by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, with Stripe Payments UK Ltd for regulated payment services). Stripe collects your payment details and billing address and, if you give one, your tax ID. We never receive or store your full card number or security code. Stripe also uses payment information for its own purposes, such as preventing fraud and meeting its legal obligations, as described in its privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Who processes information for us

Supabase runs account sign-in, including sign-in emails, our account and licence database, and the service that issues licences. The data is stored in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase, Inc. is based in the United States and may access data to provide support.

Stripe will process payments and subscriptions, as described above.

GitHub Pages hosts kaptapp.com and receives standard web request information, such as IP addresses. It is based in the United States.

When information is transferred outside the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK–US data bridge, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Account details, sign-in sessions and device records, including devices you have deactivated, are kept while your account exists and are deleted when it is deleted.

The trial record, including the hash of your email address, is kept after your account is deleted for as long as we offer free trials, so that a trial cannot be claimed again with the same email address. The hash cannot be turned back into your address.

Payment records are kept by Stripe, and we keep the payment records that UK law requires for accounting, generally for six years.

Records of payment notifications from Stripe hold Stripe IDs and processing status, not payment details, and are kept while we need them to process and check those notifications.

Browser storage on kaptapp.com

The account page keeps your signed-in session in your browser’s local storage so that you stay signed in. It is needed for the page to work and is removed when you sign out. kaptapp.com sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

Deleting your account

Email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account, sign-in sessions and device records. We keep the trial record and payment records described above. Deleting your account does not delete anything on your computer.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it, to restrict how we use it, or to give you the data you provided in a portable format. You can object to uses based on our legitimate interests. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or, if you live in the European Union, to your national data protection authority.

Children

Kapture accounts are not intended for anyone under 16, or older where local law requires it, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes to this policy

If Kapture's data practices change, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before the changed functionality is released.

If a change materially affects how we use account holders’ data, we tell them by email before it takes effect.

For privacy or support questions, email contact.
